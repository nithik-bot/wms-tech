About Us

Empowering the next generation of innovators.

Tech Innovators is a middle school STEM community designed to help students discover engineering, coding, and hands-on problem-solving in a fun, collaborative environment.

What We Build

Two main project tracks.

Every member builds toward one or both of these tracks during the school year.

Tech Challenge

We design, build, and program a machine to showcase in Tech Challenge by Tech Interactive — a season of design, testing, and redesign before showcase day.

Science Fair (New! - TBD)

Members design their own experiments, follow the engineering design process, and present their findings at the school science fair.
